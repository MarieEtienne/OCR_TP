xdm_individual_density_data.csv: This file contains the wood density values measured on increment cores from 110,763 trees, as well as tree and climate characteristics Plot_ID: Unique identifier of the forest plot inventoried by the National Forest Inventory in mainland FranceTree_ID: Identifier of the tree on the forest plotX: Longitude coordinates in decimal degrees (WGS 84) of the center of the 1 km square mesh in which the NFI forest plot was sampled (exact coordinates of NFI plots are not available due to statistical confidentiality)Y: Latitude coordinates in decimal degrees (WGS 84) of the center of the 1 km square mesh in which the NFI forest plot was sampled (exact coordinates of NFI plots are not available due to statistical confidentiality)Z: Elevation above sea level in m of the NFI forest plotBotanical_Class: Botanical class of the tree species (conifer or broadleaf)Species: Latin name of the tree species; NAs (75 cases) refer to species that could not be identified in the fieldCore_Type: Type of the sampled increment cores (pith core or peripheral core)Core_Length: Length of the sampled increment cores in green state, as estimated from the number of CT-scan images along the core, the scanner pitch (0.625 mm) and the wood core length shrinkageAge: Age of the tree in years as estimated by counting the number of tree rings on the corresponding increment core (pith cores only) offset at stem coring height (1.3 m)DBH: Stem diameter at breast height (1.3 m) in mH: Tree height in m
TM: Mean annual temperature at forest plot location in °C (normal of the 1991-2020 period provided by the French meteorological institute Météo France)
P: Total annual preciptation at forest plot location in mm (normal of the 1991-2020 period provided by the French meteorological institute Météo France)WD0: Mean anhydrous wood density in kg/m3WD0w: Weighted mean anhydrous wood density in kg/m3 WDb: Mean basic wood density in kg/m3 WDbw: Weighted mean basic wood density in kg/m3 WS: Volumetric shrinkage coefficient in % used to convert anydrous density to basic density, as gathered from literature reviewScanner_name: Name of the scanner model used for wood density measurement (GE BrightSpeed Excel or GE Revolution EVO)xdm_species_density_data.csv: This file provides statistics at species level for the weighted mean basic wood densityBotanical_Class: Botanical class of the tree species (conifer or broadleaf)Species: Latin name of the tree species Rank: Rank of the tree species according to the number of increment cores in the XyloDensMap dataset, by decreasing orderN_cores: Number of increment cores in the XyloDensMap datasetMean: Mean wood density in kg/m3Variance: Variance of wood densitySD: Standard deviation of wood density in kg/m3CV: Coefficient of variation of wood density in % (SD/mean)Mean_Inf: Lower bound of the 95% bilateral confidence interval for the mean wood density in kg/m3Mean_Sup: Upper bound of the 95% bilateral confidence interval for the mean wood density in kg/m3Mean_CI95: Range of the 95% bilateral confidence interval for the mean wood density in kg/m3Mean_Precision: Precision class around mean as estimated from the 95% bilateral confidence interval for the mean, in kg/m3SD_Inf: Lower bound of the 95% bilateral confidence interval for the standard deviation of wood density in kg/m3SD_Sup: Upper bound of the 95% bilateral confidence interval for the standard deviation of wood density in kg/m3SD_CI95: Range of the 95% bilateral confidence interval for the standard deviation of wood density in kg/m3SD_Precision: Precision class around standard deviation as estimated from the 95% bilateral confidence interval for the standard deviation, in kg/m3SW_p: P-value for the Shapiro-Wilk normality testJB_p: P-value for the Jarque-Bera normality testNormality: Indicator of acceptance or rejection of the Gaussion distribution hypothesis based on the p-values of the Shapiro-Wilk and Jarque-Bera normality tests (if SW_p or JB_p < 0.001, then rejected)xdm_shrinkage_data.csv: This file contains the wood volumetric shrinkages estimated for a subsample of increment cores from 23,828 treesPlot_ID: Unique identifier of the forest plot inventoried by the National Forest Inventory in mainland FranceTree_ID: Identifier of the tree on the forest plotBotanical_Class: Botanical class of the tree species (conifer or broadleaf)Species: Latin name of the tree species WS: Volumetric shrinkage values in % estimated using the X-ray scanner, by scanning the increment cores twice (in green and anhydrous states)XDM_R_import_and_manipulate_data.R: This file an R code designed to guide the user through data import and manipulation, with several examples of data operations and visualizations
